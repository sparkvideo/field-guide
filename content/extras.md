# The Gospel
> Every conversation is headed here.

Winning the argument saves no one. Christ does. When the door opens, walk through these four truths.

## God is holy, and He made us
➕ God created all things and made us for Himself. He is holy and just, and He will judge every person. (Gen 1:1; Isa 6:3; Acts 17:24–31; Heb 9:27)
**Ask:** *"If you died tonight and God asked why He should let you in, what would you say?"*

## We have sinned
➕ Every one of us has broken God's law. The wages of sin is death, and no amount of good works can pay it off. (Rom 3:10–12; Rom 3:23; Rom 6:23; Eph 2:1–3; Isa 64:6)
**Ask:** *"Have you kept God's law perfectly?"*

## Christ died and rose
➕ God the Son became man, lived the life we have not lived, and died in the place of sinners, bearing the judgment they deserve. He was buried and rose bodily on the third day. (John 1:14; Isa 53:5–6; 2 Cor 5:21; 1 Pet 3:18; Rom 5:8; 1 Cor 15:3–4)
**Ask:** *"Why did Jesus have to die?"*

## Repent and believe
➕ God commands all people everywhere to repent. Whoever turns from sin and trusts in Christ alone is forgiven and has eternal life: by grace, through faith, not by works. No one who comes to Him is turned away. (Mark 1:15; Acts 17:30; John 3:16; Eph 2:8–9; Rom 10:9–13; John 6:37)
**Ask:** *"What is keeping you from turning to Christ today?"*

## If they respond
➕ Pray with them. Get them a Bible and get them into a church that preaches it. Follow up. (Acts 2:41–42; Matt 28:19–20)

## If they don't
➕ Stay warm and leave the door open. You plant and water; God gives the growth. (1 Cor 3:6–7; 1 Pet 3:15–16; 2 Tim 2:24–26)

# The Trinity
> One God in three Persons.

➕ One Being, three Persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. One *what*, three *who's*. Each line below is taught in Scripture; the Trinity is what you get when you believe all of them at once.

## There is one God
➕ (Deut 6:4; Isa 43:10; Isa 44:6; 1 Cor 8:4; James 2:19)

## The Father is God
➕ (John 6:27; 1 Cor 8:6; Eph 4:6; 1 Pet 1:2)

## The Son is God
➕ (John 1:1; John 8:58; John 20:28; Col 2:9; Titus 2:13; Heb 1:8)

## The Holy Spirit is God
➕ (Acts 5:3–4; 1 Cor 3:16; Ps 139:7–8; Heb 3:7–11)
For the full argument, open [The Holy Spirit play](/holy-spirit).

## The three are distinct Persons
➕ The Father sends the Son, the Son prays to the Father, and the Spirit is sent by both. All three are present at Jesus' baptism. (Matt 3:16–17; Matt 28:19; John 14:16–17; John 17:5; 2 Cor 13:14)

## What it is not
- **Not three gods:** that is tritheism. Scripture says there is one God.
- **Not one Person in three masks:** that is modalism. The Father, Son and Spirit speak to and about one another.
- **Not a created Son or Spirit:** that is Arianism. All things were made through the Son (John 1:3).

**Ask:** *"Which of these lines do you disagree with? Let's read the verses for that one."*

# Is the Bible reliable?
> Muslims, Mormons and atheists all attack this from different sides.

## We have what they wrote
➕ The New Testament survives in thousands of Greek manuscripts, some copied within a few generations of the originals. The Dead Sea Scrolls show the Old Testament was copied faithfully for centuries before Christ. (Isa 40:8; Matt 24:35; 1 Pet 1:24–25)

## It comes from eyewitnesses
➕ The writers were eyewitnesses or wrote down what eyewitnesses told them, while many witnesses were still alive to object. (Luke 1:1–4; 2 Pet 1:16; 1 John 1:1–3; 1 Cor 15:3–8)

## Jesus treated Scripture as God's word
➕ He quoted it as final, said it cannot be broken, and said every part of it would be fulfilled. (Matt 4:4; Matt 5:17–18; John 10:35; Luke 24:44)

## God breathed it out
➕ Men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit. (2 Tim 3:16–17; 2 Pet 1:20–21)

## Prophecy written centuries ahead
➕ The suffering Servant, the pierced hands and feet, the birthplace of the Messiah and the pierced One were all written long before Jesus was born. (Isa 53; Ps 22; Mic 5:2; Zech 12:10)

# Words they use
> A quick lookup for terms that come up in conversation.

## Jehovah's Witness
- **Jehovah:** ➕ their preferred form of God's name (YHWH). For them it names the Father only.
- **New World Translation (NWT):** ➕ the Watchtower's own Bible translation.
- **Watchtower / Governing Body:** ➕ the leadership that directs all Witness teaching and calls itself the "faithful and discreet slave."
- **Active force:** ➕ their term for the Holy Spirit, whom they say is not a person.
- **Kingdom Interlinear:** ➕ the Watchtower's own Greek and English New Testament. Useful because it shows the Greek under their translation.
- **The 144,000:** ➕ the "anointed" who they say go to heaven. Everyone else hopes for life on a paradise earth.

## Unitarian
- **Biblical Unitarian:** ➕ holds that God is one person, the Father, and Jesus is a human Messiah.
- **Arian:** ➕ holds that the Son is God's first and greatest creature.
- **Shaliach (agency):** ➕ the Jewish idea that a sent agent stands for the one who sent him. They use it to explain away texts where Jesus speaks as God.
- **Oneness / modalism:** ➕ one Person who appears as Father, Son and Spirit at different times.
- **Homoousios:** ➕ "of the same essence," the Nicene term for the Son's relation to the Father.
- **Malak / angelos:** ➕ Hebrew and Greek for "messenger." It names a job, not a nature.

## Mormon
- **Godhead:** ➕ for them, three separate beings who are one in purpose.
- **Exaltation:** ➕ the teaching that faithful people can become gods.
- **Great Apostasy:** ➕ their claim that the true church was lost after the apostles.
- **Restoration:** ➕ their claim that Joseph Smith restored the church.
- **Standard works:** ➕ their four books of scripture: the Bible, the Book of Mormon, the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C), and the Pearl of Great Price.
- **JST:** ➕ the Joseph Smith Translation, his revision of the Bible.
- **Testimony / burning in the bosom:** ➕ an inner feeling taken as God's confirmation that the church is true.
- **King Follett discourse:** ➕ Joseph Smith's 1844 sermon teaching that God was once a man.

## Muslim
- **Tawhid:** ➕ the absolute oneness of Allah.
- **Shirk:** ➕ associating anything with Allah. The unforgivable sin.
- **Isa:** ➕ the Quran's name for Jesus.
- **Tawrat, Zabur, Injil:** ➕ the Torah, the Psalms and the Gospel, which the Quran says Allah revealed.
- **Tahrif:** ➕ the claim that the earlier Scriptures were corrupted.
- **Hadith:** ➕ reports of what Muhammad said and did.
- **Surah / ayah:** ➕ a chapter and a verse of the Quran.
- **Tafsir:** ➕ commentary on the Quran.
- **Hafs, Warsh, Qalun:** ➕ different transmitted readings of the Quran.

## Atheist
- **Atheist:** ➕ does not believe in God. Some go further and say there is no God.
- **Agnostic:** ➕ says they don't know, or that no one can know.
- **Naturalism:** ➕ the view that nature is all there is.
- **Objective morality:** ➕ right and wrong that are true whatever anyone thinks.
- **Burden of proof:** ➕ whoever makes a claim has to back it up. "There is no God" is a claim.

## Words we use
- **Trinity:** one God in three Persons. See [The Trinity](/topic/the-trinity).
- **Two natures:** ➕ Jesus is one Person who is truly God and truly man.
- **Monogenes:** ➕ Greek for "one of a kind," often translated "only begotten."
- **Prototokos:** ➕ Greek for "firstborn," meaning the preeminent heir.
- **Arche:** ➕ Greek for "beginning," "origin" or "ruler."
